Specifications

The materials and processes behind every GMP hammer and bit — because quality in drilling tools is the difference between a completed borewell and a stuck rig.

  • EN24 / EN36 Alloy Steel

    High-tensile nickel-chromium-molybdenum alloy steel for maximum impact resistance and fatigue life. Not mild steel, not carbon steel — the same grade used by leading global manufacturers.

  • YG8 / YG11C Tungsten Carbide

    Carbide buttons graded for the right balance of hardness and toughness, with spherical or ballistic shapes to suit your rock.

  • Heat-Treated Components

    Hammer components are heat-treated for a hard, wear-resistant surface over a tough core, so they stand up to continuous impact.

  • API-Standard Threading

    Top subs use API Reg pin threads (2-3/8", 3½", 4½") to fit standard drill pipe. Bits are made with DHD, QL, SD or Mission shanks to fit the hammer you already run.

  • Matched Hammer & Bit Range

    Every GMP bit is listed against the GMP hammers and the shank standards it fits, so ordering the right replacement is simple — see the shank guide in our catalogue.
